          FAQ

          Quick answers

          Still stuck? Email support@cutlineclip.com.

          When is auto upload coming?

          Soon. We're getting approval from YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok to post for you. Until then, export your clip, select Save clip, and upload it to any app yourself.

          What happens after my 5 free clips?

          Buy coins to make more clips, 1 coin per clip, or choose a monthly plan.

          Can I paste a YouTube or Twitch link?

          Yes. Select Add link and paste a YouTube, Twitch, or Vimeo link to watch it and mark your cuts right in Cutline. Those sites don't let other apps save their videos, so to export a finished clip, upload the file or paste a Google Drive or Dropbox link. Drive and Dropbox links open and export just like an upload. Only use videos you own, have permission to use, or received through a campaign that allows clipping.

          Which browsers work best?

          Use the latest Chrome or Edge on a computer. Keep the tab open while your clip exports, because it takes as long as the clip plays.

          Does Cutline guarantee views or payouts?

          No. Views depend on your clips and TikTok's recommendations, and each campaign decides which views it pays for.
