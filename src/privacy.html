Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

The short version: we collect what we need to run Cutline: your email, the clips you choose to post, and access to the social accounts you connect. We don't sell your information or show you ads. Your full videos are processed in your browser and aren't uploaded to us.

1. Who we are

Cutline ("we," "us") runs cutlineclip.com, a tool that turns videos into vertical clips and posts them to the social accounts you connect. Questions about this policy go to support@cutlineclip.com.

2. Information we collect

Your account

Your email address and password. We store your password only as a one-way scrambled value (a hash), so nobody at Cutline can see it. We also record when you agreed to upload only videos you have permission to use.

Your videos and clips

When you open a video in the editor, it's processed inside your browser and isn't uploaded to us. When you add a finished clip to your upload queue, that clip is uploaded to our servers so we can post it for you, along with the caption, posting time, and settings you chose.

Connected accounts

When you connect YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok, that platform gives us an access key for your account and basic profile details, like your channel name or username and account ID. We store access keys encrypted and use them only to post the clips you queue and to show which account is connected.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive a confirmation of what you bought and how much you paid. We never see or store your full card number.

Technical information

We use one cookie to keep you logged in. We record IP addresses to protect accounts, for example to block repeated wrong passwords. Your browser also stores a few preferences on your device, like which example stream you saw last.

Messages

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

3. How we use your information

  • To run Cutline: your account, your clip limit and coins, and your upload queue
  • To post the clips you queue, to the accounts you choose, at the times you choose
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To keep Cutline secure and fix problems
  • To answer your messages and send important account emails, like password resets
  • To follow the law and respond to valid legal requests

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • The platforms you choose. When a clip in your queue posts, YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok receives the clip, caption, and settings.
  • Service providers that help us run Cutline, such as our hosting provider, Stripe for payments, and our email provider. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. If the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or rights.
  • A business change. If Cutline is sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner under this policy.

5. YouTube, Google, Instagram, and TikTok

Cutline uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. With your permission, Cutline reads your channel's name and ID and uploads the videos you queue. Cutline's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Instagram is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy, and TikTok by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect any account from the Auto upload box in Cutline. When you do, we delete its stored access keys right away. You can also remove Cutline's access from your Google account permissions, your Instagram settings under Apps and websites, or TikTok's Security settings.

6. How long we keep it

  • Clip files: deleted from our servers about 3 days after you export them, unless they're still waiting in your upload queue. Then they're deleted after posting.
  • Account information: kept until you delete your account.
  • Posting history and purchase records: kept while your account is open, and purchase records as long as tax and accounting laws require.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we have about you. Email support@cutlineclip.com from the address on your account and we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, such as California or the European Union, you may have more rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, scrambled passwords, encrypted access keys, and limits on login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

9. Children

Cutline isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell you by email or on the site before they take effect.

11. Contact

Email support@cutlineclip.com.
