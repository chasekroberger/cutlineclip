      Questions clippers ask

      How does auto upload work?

      Connect your YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok account. Every clip you export uploads to YouTube and Instagram automatically. TikTok requires you to review and confirm each post, so it takes one tap.

      Which Instagram accounts work?

      Instagram only lets apps post to Business or Creator accounts. You can switch for free in the Instagram app under Settings, Account type and tools.

      What happens after my 5 free clips?

      Buy coins to make more clips, 1 coin per clip, or choose a monthly plan.

      Can I paste a YouTube or Twitch link?

      Yes. Select Add link and paste it. Direct video links load right away, and YouTube, Twitch, and similar links import through your Cutline account. Only use videos you own, have permission to use, or received through a campaign that allows clipping.

      Which browsers work best?

      Use the latest Chrome or Edge on a computer. Keep the tab open while your clip exports, because it takes as long as the clip plays.

      Does Cutline guarantee views or payouts?

      No. Views depend on your clips and TikTok's recommendations, and each campaign decides which views it pays for.
